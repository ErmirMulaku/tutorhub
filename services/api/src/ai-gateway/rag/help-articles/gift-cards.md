title: Gift Cards
---

TutorHub gift cards are a simple way to pay for lessons in advance or give someone else credit to book with.

Gift cards are sold in fixed denominations from the TutorHub store. When you buy one, you receive a code by email that can be redeemed by any TutorHub account — your own, or someone else's if you're gifting it.

**Redeeming a gift card.** Enter the code on your account's wallet page. The full value is added to your TutorHub wallet balance immediately; there's no partial redemption or expiry date on the credit once it's in your wallet. A gift card code can only be redeemed once.

**Spending gift-card credit.** Once redeemed, gift-card credit is indistinguishable from any other wallet balance — it's spent the same way, automatically applied to bookings before your card is charged for any remaining amount. See the payment methods article for how wallet and card payments combine.

**Refunds and gift cards.** Gift cards themselves are non-refundable and can't be exchanged for cash — this is standard for gift cards generally. However, if you book a lesson using redeemed gift-card credit and later cancel under the cancellation policy, the refunded amount goes back to your wallet as ordinary wallet balance, which you can still use for future bookings.

Gift cards don't expire, and unused wallet credit never expires either, so there's no rush to spend it.
