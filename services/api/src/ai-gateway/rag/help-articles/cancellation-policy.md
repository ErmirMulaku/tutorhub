title: Cancellation Policy
---

TutorHub's cancellation policy balances flexibility for students with fair treatment of tutors who reserve time for a lesson.

**Free cancellation until 24 hours before the lesson.** If you cancel a booking more than 24 hours before its scheduled start time, you receive a full refund to your original payment method (or wallet, if that's how you paid) with no fee at all. Cancel from "My Bookings" — the refund is processed automatically.

**50% fee within 24 hours of the lesson.** If you cancel less than 24 hours before the scheduled start time, you're charged a 50% cancellation fee and refunded the remaining 50%. This reflects the fact that your tutor held that slot and, this close to the lesson, usually can't fill it with another student.

**No-shows are charged in full.** If you don't cancel and simply don't show up for the lesson, no refund is issued — the full lesson price is charged. The same applies if you join more than 15 minutes late without notifying your tutor; the lesson is marked as a no-show.

If a tutor cancels on you — for any reason, at any notice — you always receive a full refund with no fee, regardless of how close to the lesson time it is. Tutors cancelling repeatedly or with little notice may face account review.

Refunds under this policy follow the same timeline as refunds generally — see the refunds article for how long they take to reach you.

Rescheduling has its own rules, closely mirroring this policy — see the rescheduling article.
