title: Reviews
---

Reviews help future students pick the right tutor, and they only come from students who actually had a lesson.

**Who can leave a review.** Only the student who booked a lesson can review it, and only after the lesson is marked completed. You can't review a tutor you haven't had a lesson with, and you can't review a lesson that was cancelled rather than completed.

**What a review contains.** A review is a 1–5 star rating plus an optional written comment. Both are shown on the tutor's public profile, alongside their average rating across all reviews. There's no separate rating for things like punctuality or communication — it's a single overall score per lesson.

**Editing and removing reviews.** You can edit your review's rating or comment for a short period after posting it. Tutors can't edit or delete a student's review, but they can report one that violates TutorHub's content guidelines (for example, if it's abusive or doesn't relate to the actual lesson) for the support team to review.

**How reviews affect a tutor's profile.** A tutor's average rating is a straightforward average of all their reviews — there's no weighting by recency or lesson length. New tutors with few reviews show their actual review count, so students can judge how established the rating is.

Reviews are tied to a specific booking, so a single lesson can only generate one review, even if you've had multiple lessons with the same tutor.
