title: Tutor Payouts
---

Tutors get paid for lessons through Stripe Connect, not through a manual invoice process.

**Setting up payouts.** When you're approved as a tutor, you'll be prompted to connect a Stripe Express account — this is where TutorHub sends your earnings. It takes a few minutes: Stripe collects your bank details and basic tax information directly, and TutorHub never sees or stores your bank account number.

**When you get paid.** A lesson's earnings become available for payout once the lesson is marked completed. TutorHub pays out automatically on a weekly schedule to your connected Stripe account; you don't need to request a payout manually. You can see upcoming and past payouts, and which lessons they cover, from your tutor dashboard's earnings page.

**Platform fee.** TutorHub deducts a platform fee from each lesson's price before the remainder is transferred to you — the amount a student pays is not the same as the amount a tutor receives. Your dashboard breaks this out per lesson, so you can see the gross price, the fee, and your net payout separately.

**Cancelled and refunded lessons.** If a lesson is cancelled with a full refund (by either you or the student, under the cancellation policy), there's no payout for it — it never counted as earnings in the first place. A 50% late cancellation by a student still pays you for the 50% that wasn't refunded.

If your Stripe Express account isn't fully set up, payouts are held until it is — you'll see a reminder on your dashboard if anything's missing.
