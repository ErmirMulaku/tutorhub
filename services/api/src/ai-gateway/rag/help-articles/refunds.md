---

Refunds on TutorHub are issued automatically whenever you're entitled to one under the cancellation policy — you don't need to contact support to request a standard cancellation refund.

**Where the refund goes.** If you paid by card, the refund goes back to that same card via Stripe. If you paid using your TutorHub wallet balance, the refund is credited straight back to your wallet. If you paid with a mix of wallet and card, the wallet portion is refunded to your wallet first, then any remaining amount goes back to your card.

**How long it takes.** Wallet refunds are instant — you'll see the updated balance immediately. Card refunds are issued immediately on TutorHub's side but can take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer; this delay is standard for card networks, not something TutorHub controls.

**What's refunded.** A cancellation more than 24 hours before the lesson refunds 100% of what you paid. A cancellation within 24 hours refunds 50%, per the cancellation policy. No-shows are not refunded. If a tutor cancels, you always receive a 100% refund regardless of timing.

**Gift cards.** Amounts paid using redeemed gift-card credit (held in your wallet) are refunded to your wallet, not back to a gift card — gift cards themselves are non-refundable once redeemed, but the wallet credit they created can still come back to you.

If a refund doesn't appear after 10 business days, contact support with your booking reference so we can check the Stripe transaction directly.
