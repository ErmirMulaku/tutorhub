title: Becoming a Tutor
---

Anyone with teaching experience in a subject can apply to become a TutorHub tutor.

**Applying.** Start from "Become a Tutor" on the marketing site. You'll create a profile — name, bio, languages, the subjects you want to teach, and your hourly rate per subject. You'll also set your timezone and your regular working hours, which later power the availability calendar students book against.

**Verification.** Before your profile goes live, TutorHub reviews your application. This includes basic identity verification and a look at the subjects and experience you've listed. Most applications are reviewed within a few business days. You'll get an email either way — approved applications go live immediately, and if we need more information, we'll ask for it rather than rejecting outright.

**Setting availability.** Once approved, you set your working hours per weekday (for example, weekdays 9am–5pm in your local timezone). The platform automatically turns this into bookable slots, accounting for existing bookings, buffers between lessons, and any breaks you've configured, so you never have to manually block out time for lessons you already have.

**Getting bookings.** Your profile appears in search and subject browsing once it's live. Students can message you before booking to ask questions, and once they book, the lesson appears on your tutor dashboard alongside your other bookings.

For how you actually get paid for completed lessons, see the tutor payouts article.
