title: Account & Privacy
---

Your TutorHub account holds the information needed to book lessons, message tutors, and manage payments — and only that.

**What TutorHub stores.** Your profile (name, email, optional phone number and avatar), your booking and message history, your wallet balance, and references to your payments. TutorHub does not store your full card number — that's held by Stripe, and TutorHub only keeps a reference to each transaction. Tutors' bank details are similarly held by Stripe, not by TutorHub directly.

**Signing in.** Accounts are protected by a password you set at signup, or in some environments a social sign-in (Google or Apple) that verifies your identity with the provider rather than a TutorHub password. Email addresses are verified at signup before the account can book lessons or receive payouts.

**Who can see your information.** Other users only see what's on your public profile (for students, just your name and avatar in conversations and reviews; for tutors, your full public profile). Your email, phone number, and payment details are never shown to other users, including the tutor or student you're interacting with.

**Deleting your account.** You can request account deletion from your account settings. This removes your profile and personal information; records required for financial or legal reasons (like completed transaction history) may be retained in anonymized form as required by law.

**Changing your information.** You can update your name, avatar, phone number, and password at any time from your account page. Email changes may require re-verifying the new address.
