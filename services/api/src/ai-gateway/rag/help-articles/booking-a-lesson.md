title: Booking a Lesson
---

Booking a lesson on TutorHub takes a few minutes. Start by browsing or searching for tutors by subject — every tutor profile lists their subjects, hourly rate, languages, and a short bio so you can find a good fit before committing to anything.

Once you've picked a tutor, open their availability calendar. Tutors set their own working hours, and the calendar only shows slots that are actually free — TutorHub checks existing bookings and working-hours breaks automatically, so you can't select a time the tutor can't take. Pick a subject and a time slot, then review the price before confirming.

Confirming a booking charges your selected payment method immediately — a credit or debit card via Stripe, your TutorHub wallet balance, or a combination of both if your wallet doesn't cover the full price. The lesson is confirmed as soon as payment succeeds; you'll see it in "My Bookings" along with a reminder before it starts.

If a tutor doesn't respond to messages or the time slot you wanted disappears while you're deciding, that's normal — availability updates in real time as other students book the same tutor. Refresh the calendar if a slot you were looking at is no longer there.

Once booked, you can reschedule or cancel from "My Bookings" — see the separate articles on rescheduling and the cancellation policy for how fees work. You can also message your tutor directly from the booking to ask questions before the lesson starts.
