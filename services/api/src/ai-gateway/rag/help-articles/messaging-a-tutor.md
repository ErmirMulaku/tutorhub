title: Messaging a Tutor
---

TutorHub has in-app messaging so you can talk to a tutor before — or after — booking a lesson.

**Starting a conversation.** You can message a tutor directly from their profile, even before you've booked anything. This is useful for asking about their teaching style, confirming they can cover a specific topic, or checking availability that isn't reflected on the calendar yet. Each student-tutor pair has a single ongoing conversation thread, rather than a separate thread per booking.

**What messaging is for.** Messaging is meant for lesson-related questions — scheduling details, what to prepare, following up after a lesson. It isn't a substitute for booking: a tutor can't confirm a paid lesson through chat alone, and payment always goes through the normal booking flow so that the cancellation policy and refunds apply properly.

**Notifications.** Both students and tutors get notified of new messages. Unread messages are counted and shown on the conversation list so you can see at a glance what needs a reply.

**Privacy.** Messages are only visible to the two people in the conversation — TutorHub staff can access them only to investigate a reported issue. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information like full card numbers or passwords in chat; TutorHub never asks for this over messaging, and support will never ask for your password this way either.

If a tutor doesn't respond, that's independent of booking — their calendar availability is still accurate even if they're slow to reply to messages.
