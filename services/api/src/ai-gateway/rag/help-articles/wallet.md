title: Your TutorHub Wallet
---

Every TutorHub account has a wallet — a balance, tracked in cents, that you can spend on lessons but can't withdraw as cash.

**How the wallet gets funded.** There are two ways money lands in your wallet: refunds (a cancellation refund, whether 100% or the 50% portion under the cancellation policy, goes to your wallet if that's how you originally paid) and redeemed gift cards (the full value of a gift card code is credited to your wallet the moment you redeem it).

**How the wallet is spent.** When you book a lesson, you can choose to pay from your wallet. If your balance covers the full price, nothing is charged to your card. If it only covers part of the price, TutorHub automatically charges your card for the rest — you'll see both amounts broken out at checkout so there are no surprises.

**What the wallet can't do.** Wallet balance can't be transferred to another account, withdrawn to a bank account, or converted back into a gift card. It exists only to be spent on TutorHub lessons. There's no expiry on wallet balance — it stays on your account until you use it.

**Checking your balance.** Your current wallet balance is always visible on your account page, along with a history of what added to it (refunds, gift-card redemptions) and what it's been spent on.

If a wallet-funded booking is later cancelled or refunded, the money returns to your wallet rather than to a card, since that's where it came from.
