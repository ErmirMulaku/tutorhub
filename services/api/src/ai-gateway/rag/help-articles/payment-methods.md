title: Payment Methods
---

TutorHub accepts a few ways to pay for lessons, and you can mix them on a single booking.

**Credit or debit card.** Card payments are processed through Stripe. TutorHub never stores your full card number — Stripe handles the card details directly, and TutorHub only keeps a reference to the payment. You can save a card for faster checkout or enter one fresh each time.

**Wallet balance.** Every account has a TutorHub wallet, shown in cents on your account page. Your wallet is funded by refunds and by redeeming gift cards — you can't deposit cash directly into it. When you book a lesson, you can choose to pay from your wallet first; if your wallet balance doesn't cover the full price, the remainder is automatically charged to your card.

**Gift cards.** Gift cards are purchased separately and redeemed into your wallet as credit — see the gift cards article for details. Once redeemed, that credit spends exactly like any other wallet balance.

**What gets charged, and when.** Your payment method is charged at the moment you confirm a booking, not when the lesson happens. If you cancel under the free-cancellation window, that charge is refunded in full; within 24 hours, 50% is refunded per the cancellation policy.

You can view your full payment history, including which method was used for each booking, from "My Bookings." If a card payment fails at checkout, the booking isn't created — no lesson is held until payment succeeds.
