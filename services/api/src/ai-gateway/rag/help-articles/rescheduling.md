title: Rescheduling a Lesson
---

Plans change, and TutorHub makes rescheduling straightforward as long as you do it with enough notice.

If a lesson is more than 24 hours away, you can reschedule it for free from "My Bookings." Pick a new slot from the tutor's current availability — the same calendar you used to book originally — and your lesson moves to the new time with no extra charge. Your tutor is notified automatically.

If a lesson is less than 24 hours away, rescheduling is treated the same as cancelling and booking a new lesson: the cancellation policy's 50% fee applies to the original booking, and you'll need to pay for the new slot separately. This exists because a tutor who held a slot for you within the 24-hour window usually can't fill it with another student in time, so the same fee logic that protects tutors from last-minute cancellations also applies to last-minute reschedules.

There's no limit on how many times you can reschedule a lesson outside the 24-hour window, but please be considerate of your tutor's time — repeatedly rescheduling the same lesson at the last minute may be flagged.

If your tutor needs to reschedule (for example, they become unavailable), you'll be notified and offered alternative times at no cost to you; you're also free to cancel instead and receive a full refund in that case, since the change wasn't something you initiated.

For full cancellations rather than rescheduling, see the cancellation policy article.
